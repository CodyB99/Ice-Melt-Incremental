# Content Catalog

## Zones

### 1. Frozen Backyard
Bright, friendly onboarding area. Snow-covered lawn, frozen kiddie pool, patio, toys, grill, shed, and shallow pond. Ice is thin and readable.

### 2. Ice Cavern
Blue crystal cave with fossils, frozen chests, mineral clusters, narrow bridges, and warm lava cracks in the distance. Strong contrast and echoing audio.

### 3. Frozen City
Frozen street grid with cars, lamps, storefronts, hydrants, bus stop, plaza, and large set-piece ice clusters. The skyline should sell progress without requiring enterable buildings.

Rarity baseline:

- Common 55%
- Uncommon 25%
- Rare 12%
- Epic 5.5%
- Legendary 2%
- Secret 0.5%

Weights are adjusted by zone and luck, then normalized. A player cannot lose a discovery because of UI closure or disconnect after the server awards it.

## Chain reaction objects

- Heat Pocket — melts a small nearby cluster.
- Lava Capsule — larger circular chain.
- Unstable Reactor — rare city chain with server announcement.

Chain reactions award normal validated rewards with a hard server cap per event.
