# UI, UX, and Onboarding

## HUD hierarchy

Top/upper area:

- Heat balance with animated delta
- Ember balance after first Thaw becomes relevant
- current zone and next goal

Bottom/right thumb-safe area:

- Upgrades
- Tools
- Zones
- Discoveries
- Shop

Avoid covering the character and immediate melt radius on phone screens.

## First-session onboarding

1. Camera points toward nearby ice.
2. Prompt: `Walk into the ice to melt it.`
3. On first melt, highlight Heat counter.
4. When first upgrade is affordable, highlight Power Upgrade.
5. On purchase, show stronger cracks and a short comparison burst.
6. Introduce tool unlock only after the player has repeated the loop.
7. Introduce Thaw only when progress is meaningful.

No modal tutorial wall. Each step is event-driven, skippable after comprehension, and saved.

## Feedback stack

For normal melt:

- crack decal/stage or material change
- short ice crack + soft hiss
- steam/spark particle
- compact Heat popup
- subtle scale/dissolve

For rare discovery:

- rarity-colored frame/effect with accessibility icon/text
- distinct sting
- collection reveal card
- server announcement only for Legendary/Secret

For chain reaction:

- brief anticipation pulse
- expanding wave
- batched sound layers
- optional mild camera shake with setting toggle

## Accessibility/settings

- reduced motion
- camera shake on/off
- music and SFX sliders
- effects quality: low/medium/high/auto
- color-independent rarity labels and icons
- UI scale support
- controller selection states

## Shop safety

Close buttons must be obvious. Purchase buttons display the live Robux price and clearly state duration/permanence.
